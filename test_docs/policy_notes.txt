Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with prior approval from their manager. This policy is designed to support work-life balance while maintaining productivity.

Guidelines for Remote Work:

- **Approval Process**: Submit your remote work request to your manager at least 48 hours in advance.
- **Availability**: Be reachable via email, phone, or messaging platforms during standard working hours.
- **Meetings**: Attend all mandatory virtual or in-person meetings unless otherwise excused.
- **Workspace**: Maintain a safe and ergonomic home workspace free from distractions.
- **Company Equipment**: Use company laptops and devices in accordance with IT security policies. Avoid using public Wi-Fi for confidential work unless connected via VPN.
- **Data Security**: Protect sensitive company information by following data handling protocols while working remotely.
- **Performance**: Ensure remote work does not negatively impact your work quality or deadlines.
- **Support**: Contact IT support for help with technical issues during remote work days.
- **Compliance**: Employees must comply with all company policies and local labor laws while working remotely.

Violation of the remote work policy may result in revocation of remote work privileges or other disciplinary actions.

For questions or clarification, reach out to your manager or HR department.

We encourage employees to make the most of remote work opportunities responsibly and productively.
